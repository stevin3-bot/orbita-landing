Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

In short: we keep the minimum for your account to work: your email, the status of your trial or subscription, how many minutes and days you use Órbita, and which computers use it. We never see your code, your files, what you say to the AIs or your voice. No analytics, no ads, no selling of data.

Steven José López Sandí, an individual based in Costa Rica, is responsible for the data described here. Contact: stevenlopezsandi3@gmail.com.

Payments are handled by Paddle as Merchant of Record: payment and billing data are processed by Paddle under its privacy policy.

3. What we don't collect

  • Your code, files and projects: they stay on your computer.
  • What you say to the AI agents: it goes straight from your computer to each agent's provider (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google or others), with your own account and under their privacy policy. It doesn't go through our servers.
  • Your voice: dictation is processed on your computer. Only the first time is the speech model downloaded from Hugging Face.
  • Analytics, tracking or advertising: Órbita has none. We don't sell or rent data to anyone.

4. Who it's shared with

Only with the providers we need to run the service, which process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase (servers in the United States): the accounts and licenses database, and sign-in.
  • Brevo (European Union): sending sign-in code emails and internal feedback notices.
  • Paddle (United Kingdom): payments, invoices and taxes, as Merchant of Record.
  • GitHub (United States): downloading Órbita and its updates; GitHub sees your IP address when you download.

Some of these providers are outside your country. We use providers that offer adequate safeguards (such as standard contractual clauses) for those transfers.

5. How long we keep it

  • Account, license and computer data: while you have an account. If it isn't used for 24 months, we delete it.
  • Days of use: only those needed for the extra-days rule and the computer limit.
  • Feedback: up to 24 months.
  • Payment data and invoices are kept by Paddle for as long as the law requires.

6. Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data, object to or restrict its processing, or receive a copy, by writing to stevenlopezsandi3@gmail.com. We'll reply within 30 days. Deleting your account doesn't automatically cancel a running subscription: cancel it first from Paddle's portal.

If you live in Costa Rica you may complain to the Agencia de Protección de Datos de los Habitantes (PRODHAB); in the European Union, to your country's data protection authority.

7. Security

Communications are encrypted (HTTPS). On your computer, the session is stored encrypted with the operating system's secure storage, and the license is signed so it can't be altered. On our servers, only the service's own functions can read the account tables.

8. Children

Órbita isn't intended for people under 18 and we don't knowingly collect their data.

9. Changes

If we change this policy significantly, we'll let you know by email or in the app before it applies.
